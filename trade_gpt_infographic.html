The 12-Gate Confluence Framework

Every trade signal must clear 12 independent validation checkpoints before execution. No exceptions. No overrides.

Phase A — Technical Foundation (Gates 1–6)
1
Confluence Score
Weighted blend of RSI, MACD, EMA, Bollinger Bands, Stochastic & 4H Bias. Must reach ≥45% to proceed.
2
SMC Structure
Scans for BOS, ChoCH, Fair Value Gaps, Order Blocks & Liquidity Sweeps. At least 1 must be present.
3
HTF Trend Guard
4-Hour RSI bias must align with trade direction. Never trade counter-trend.
4
Session Filter
Only executes during high-volume windows (London & NY overlap). No Asia chop.
5
Volatility Check
ATR ratio vs 20-period avg must be between 0.5× and 2.5×. Blocks dead or erratic markets.
6
No Conflict Guard
Bull vs bear indicator weight spread must exceed 10%. No entry on mixed signals.
Phase B — Risk & Event Filters (Gates 7–9)
7
Asset Cooldown
4-hour freeze per symbol after execution. Prevents destructive over-trading.
8
Economic News Block
Blocks 30min before and 15min after NFP, FOMC, CPI, ECB, BoJ events via live Finnhub calendar.
9
Correlation Guard
Validates correlated assets (Gold/DXY, BTC/ETH) agree with trade direction.
Phase C — Precision Entry (Gates 10–12)
10
MTF Bias Stack
Weekly, Daily & 4H EMA directions checked. 2 of 3 timeframes must agree.
11
VWAP Deviation
Price must not be beyond ±2σ VWAP bands. Prevents buying tops or selling bottoms.
12
Candlestick Entry
1H wick/body patterns validated: Pin Bars, Hammers, Engulfing candles required.
Execute Signal
8 or more gates passed — BUY or SELL order dispatched to broker via MetaAPI
Add to Watchlist
5–7 gates passed — monitor for improving conditions, re-evaluate next candle
Discard Setup
Fewer than 5 gates passed — no signal issued, capital preserved

Gating Outcomes
Signal / Watch / No_Trade
After the 12-gate scan, the engine returns one of three outcomes:
  • SIGNAL — 4+ gates passed, confluence ≥ 45%, news clear, MTF aligned → full trade ticket generated
  • WATCH — Partial confluence. Setup has potential but missing confirmations. Monitor and retry later
  • NO_TRADE — Critical gate failed (confluence, news, or MTF). Entry blocked to protect capital

What's Inside the Ticket
When a SIGNAL outcome is returned, the trade ticket includes:
  • Symbol + Action — e.g. XAUUSD BUY with instrument-specific icon

  • Required Margin — Approximate capital locked for the trade
  • Projected Risk / Profit — Dollar amounts for SL and TP hit scenarios
  • Confidence Grade — AAA, AA, A, or BBB with visual bar

§1.0 Retail Disadvantage & Platform Thesis
Retail trading platforms typically operate in isolation, providing basic charts and indicator overlays. However, institutional trading relies heavily on multi-variable quantitative models that analyze order book depth, liquidity voids, and macroeconomic event timings.
The core thesis of TradeGPT is to eliminate this systemic information asymmetry. By running a server-side confluence pipeline, the system scans watchlist symbols every 15 minutes across multiple timeframes, validating structure against raw liquidity pools.
Rather than holding client funds or acting as a counterparty, the platform operates as a pure software execution bridge. Trades are routed directly to client-owned MT4/MT5 accounts, protecting traders from custodial risks and counterparty insolvency.

§10.0 Multi-Timeframe Bias Stack
To isolate high-probability setups from low-timeframe market noise, the engine implements a multi-timeframe bias validation model via mtfBias.ts. Trading signals are filtered using a weighted directional composite of Exponential Moving Averages (EMA 20, 50, 100, 200).
Formula 4: MTF Directional Score Composite Bias (Bc) = ωWBW + ωDBD + ω4HB4H
ωW = 0.50 (Weekly)  ·  ωD = 0.30 (Daily)  ·  ω4H = 0.20 (4H)
Bi ∈ {-1 (Bearish), 0 (Neutral), +1 (Bullish)}
A signal is rejected if the Directional Score conflicts with the entry direction. For example, a **BUY** order ticket is blocked if $B_c < 0.20$, preventing entries against structural higher-timeframe order flow.
§11.0 Webhook Signature Integrity
Rebate and withdrawal settlements rely on webhooks from payment gateways. To prevent replay attacks and invoice spoofing, the node backend verifies payload signatures using the HMAC-SHA512 standard.
// Node.js Webhook Signature Validator import crypto from 'crypto'; export function verifyWebhookIPN( rawPayload: string, receivedSignature: string, secretKey: string ): boolean { const hmac = crypto.createHmac('sha512', secretKey); const calculatedSignature = hmac.update(rawPayload).digest('hex'); // Constant-time comparison to prevent timing attacks return crypto.timingSafeEqual( Buffer.from(calculatedSignature, 'hex'), Buffer.from(receivedSignature, 'hex') ); }

§12.0 Algorithmic Risk Containment Rules
Slippage & Latency
Execution delays over WAN networks can result in entry slippage, particularly during low-liquidity market rollovers (21:58 - 22:15 UTC).
Failover Delay
Switching between Twelve Data and Yahoo Finance takes up to 3 seconds, during which tick updates are suspended in the watchlist.
Correlation Decay
Currency relationships evaluated by the correlation engine can decouple during high-volatility events, potentially generating false confluences.
Circuit Breakers
If MetaAPI registers three consecutive connection errors on a terminal node, the auto-execution process for that account is deactivated.

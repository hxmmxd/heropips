Outbound Telecalling & Ad Optimization Strategy
📞
High efficiency telecalling. By building our 10-person telecalling desk locally in Lucknow, staff salaries are kept at competitive Tier-2 market rates (averaging ₹15,000/mo). Telecallers target outbound lists from active trading channels, delivering a high volume of direct beta app downloads at low customer acquisition costs (CAC).
📈
Meta Lead & App Install Campaigns. Meta Ads (₹20,000 – ₹40,000/mo) are optimized strictly for custom app-install tracking, targeting retail forex and equity traders. Leads generated flow directly into a custom CRM dialer software, allowing telecallers to follow up in real-time, driving active users straight into subscription pipelines.
🏢
Lean Office Infrastructure. By choosing a rental space providing standard utilities where the landlord covers structural expenses (such as the AC system), we minimize our initial CapEx setup to just $1,000. This protects our runway, enabling us to easily test our beta model before upgrading to premium facilities.
Operations & Team Scaling Notes

📌 Establishing the Operational Engine

  • Why Lucknow? Setting up the operations hub in Lucknow provides a massive cost advantage. Renting a 600-800 sq ft office in Gomti Nagar or Hazratganj costs just ₹20,000/mo (~$240), and hiring a team of 10 outbound call representatives (₹1,50,000/mo total) and 3 core team members (₹60,000/mo) costs only ~$2,515/mo combined in salaries, reducing our monthly runway risk by 60% compared to Tier-1 hubs.
  • Entity Structure & Legal: The Private Limited registration (₹16,000) provides legal compliance, limited liability protection, and structured equity allocations. The Import Export Code (IEC - ₹2,500) ensures we can smoothly process payments and international subscription flows.
  • Beta Conversion Funnel: The 10 telecallers are tasked with contacting forex/crypto traders to sign up for the TradeGPT beta app. The KPI target is 20 new beta registrations per caller per day, creating a steady stream of prospective active users who convert into paid subscribers at launch.
  • Consolidated Break-Even: Because the office, team, utilities, marketing spends, and scalable server infrastructure total only ~$4,366/mo, TradeGPT reaches absolute corporate break-even with just **151 paid monthly subscribers** at $29/mo, demonstrating the model's exceptional financial leverage.

[Screen: Black background. Title fades in — "Signal Intelligence"]

Ten independent analysis engines. Each one looking at the market from a completely different angle. Each one running its own calculations. And they don't talk to each other until the very end.

[Screen: Engines appear one by one as they're mentioned]

Engine one. The **Indicator Engine.** RSI using Wilder's original smoothing. MACD. A triple EMA stack — twenty, fifty, two hundred. Bollinger Bands. Stochastic. ATR. All pure TypeScript. No external libraries. No Python. No latency.

Engine two. The **Smart Money Scanner.** This one looks for what institutions leave behind. Break of Structure. Change of Character. Fair Value Gaps — those three-candle imbalances the market wants to fill. Order Blocks. Liquidity Sweeps — those wicks that hunt your stop loss before reversing. Six institutional patterns. Detected automatically.

[Pause]

Engine three. **Confluence Scoring.** A weighted directional blend. RSI contributes fifteen percent. MACD, twenty. EMAs, twenty. Bollinger Bands, fifteen. Stochastic, ten. The four-hour timeframe, fifteen. Every signal gets a directional score. If it's below forty-five percent — it's discarded.

Engine four. **Multi-Timeframe Bias.** Weekly. Daily. Four-hour. All three must align their EMA direction. If only one agrees — no signal. Two out of three must stack.

Engine five. **Divergence Detection.** Regular and hidden divergence across RSI and MACD simultaneously.

Engine six. **VWAP.** Volume-Weighted Average Price with two-sigma deviation bands. So you never buy the top. And never sell the bottom.

Engine seven. **Correlation Guard.** Gold and the Dollar should move inversely. Bitcoin and Ethereum should move together. If correlated markets disagree — no signal.

Engine eight. **The News Shield.** We scan the Finnhub economic calendar. Thirty minutes before and after NFP, FOMC, CPI, ECB, Bank of England, Bank of Japan — the system hard-locks. No signal is generated. Period.

[Pause — this is important]

Engine nine. **Candle Pattern Recognition.** Pin bars. Engulfing patterns. Hammers. Doji. Confirmed at key structural levels.

Engine ten. **Kelly Criterion Position Sizing.** The mathematically optimal lot size based on your win rate and reward ratio. Hard-capped at two percent of your account.

[Pause]

[Screen: All ten engines light up, arrows flow into the 12-Gate sequence]

Ten engines. Running independently. Producing independent conclusions.

And then — they all feed into this.

[Screen: 12 gates appear in a grid — each with a name and kill condition]

The **Twelve-Gate Validation Sequence.**

Gate four checks the trading session — London or New York only for session-specific assets. Gate five checks volatility isn't dead or extreme. Gate six checks there's no directional conflict. Gate seven enforces a four-hour cooldown per asset. Gate eight checks for high-impact news events.

## ACT 3: THE 12-GATE ENGINE — THE CORE ADVANTAGE (3:30 – 5:00)

### Visual
Animated flow diagram or screen recording of the gate validation section. Show each gate lighting up as it's explained.

> *"Here's what makes TradeGPT different from every signal bot, every Telegram group, every copy-trading platform you've ever tried.*
>
> *Before any trade is issued, it must survive 12 independent validation filters — we call them the 12 Gates."*

**[Show gates 1–4 with quick visual for each:]**

> *"Gate 1 — Confluence Score. A weighted blend of 6 technical indicators. Must score above 45% or the setup is instantly rejected.*
>
> *Gate 2 — Smart Money Concepts. The engine scans for institutional price structures — Break of Structure, Order Blocks, Fair Value Gaps. At least one must be confirmed.*
>
> *Gate 3 — Higher Timeframe Alignment. Your trade must agree with the 4-hour trend. Never trade counter to the dominant direction.*
>
> *Gate 4 — Session Filter. We only issue signals during peak liquidity — London and New York session overlap. No Asia chop."*

**[Quick flash through gates 5–8:]**

> *"Gates 5 through 8 check volatility levels, indicator conflicts, 4-hour cooldown timers, and — critically — the economic calendar. TradeGPT automatically blocks all signals within 30 minutes of high-impact news releases like NFP, FOMC, or CPI."*

**[Gates 9–12:]**

> *"Gates 9 through 12 verify cross-asset correlation — for example, Gold must align with the Dollar Index. Then it checks multi-timeframe EMA stacks, VWAP deviation bands, and finally, candlestick entry patterns like Pin Bars and Engulfing candles.*
>
> *Only setups that pass 8 or more of these 12 filters generate a live trade signal. Everything else is discarded — automatically."*

**[Visual: Show the outcome — ≥8 gates = green "EXECUTE", 5–7 = yellow "WATCHLIST", <5 = red "SKIP"]**

🧠 The Core Idea
Most trading platforms sign up users and immediately push them to deposit money and trade. 80% of those users fail, feel confused, and quit within 30 days — that's the industry churn problem. We flip this completely. Every new user, before they ever see a "Buy" button, goes through a structured 7-day education path. They arrive as a stranger. They leave as a confident, informed trader who trusts our platform.
⚡ Why This Changes Everything
A user who understands trading psychology, risk management, and the platform before paying is 3× more likely to stay beyond 3 months. Education is not just a feature — it's our primary churn-reduction tool and the reason our 8% monthly churn is half the industry average of 15–18%.
🔧 How It Works — Step by Step

User signs up free. Instantly receives "Welcome to TradeGPT" video (5 min). Explains what AI trading is, how rebates work, and what they'll earn. No pressure, no pitch.

Telecaller calls with a warm open: "You've completed your free training — did you enjoy it?" Conversion is now 3× easier because the user already trusts us. Offer: Pro plan for $29/month.

Revenue Sharing with Users
When users earn, they trade more. When they trade more, we earn more.
50% activation → $19/user/month
🧠 The Core Idea
The broker pays us a rebate for every lot our users trade. Instead of keeping all of it, we share 40% back with the user as cash rewards and bonuses. This creates a virtuous cycle: users trade more to earn more rebate. More trading = more revenue for us. Everyone wins — and no one wants to leave a platform that's paying them back.
⚡ The Math Behind It
Example: Broker pays us $8 per standard lot. User trades 10 lots = $80 gross rebate to us. We give 40% back = $32 to the user. We keep 60% = $48. But because the user got rewarded, they trade 15 lots next month → $72 to us (60% of $120). Revenue grows as reward grows.
🔧 The 3-Layer Reward Structure
Layer 1
Rebate Share (Automatic) — 40% of every rebate earned is deposited monthly into user's platform wallet. No action needed. They see it growing every day. Transparency builds addiction to the platform.
Layer 2
Volume Milestones (Bonus) — Trade 5 lots: get $50 bonus. Trade 20 lots: get $150 bonus. Trade 50 lots: get $500 bonus. These are visible targets that drive behavior — like a game level-up system.
Layer 3
Loyalty Tier Upgrades — Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum based on 3-month trading volume. Higher tiers get better rebate rates, priority support, and exclusive signals. Aspirational and sticky.

Referral Ecosystem — Multi-Level
Users become salespeople. Their network becomes our distribution.
$0 CAC referral channel by M4
🧠 The Core Idea
Every user who joins our platform is given a unique referral link. When they refer someone, they earn a percentage of that person's rebates — forever, not just for 30 days. This is the key insight: most referral programs pay a one-time bonus. Ours pays indefinitely. If your friend trades for 2 years, you earn from every single lot they trade for 2 years. That's life-changing for active referrers — and an incredibly powerful retention tool for us.
⚡ Why Permanent Earnings Change Everything
If a referred user stops trading, the referrer still has 2 other referred users earning for them. The referrer will never leave the platform because leaving means losing all their passive income. This creates a built-in retention mechanism for our most valuable users — the ones who bring others.
🔧 The 3-Level Structure — A Real Example
You (Level 0)
You're an active trader. You refer 5 friends. Each friend generates $48/month in rebates for us. Your 30% share = $14.40/month per friend = $72/month passive income just from 5 referrals.
Level 2
Each of your 5 friends refers 3 people each = 15 Level 2 users. You earn 15% of their rebates. If each generates $48: 15 × $48 × 15% = +$108/month additional earnings — from people you never even met.
Level 3
Each Level 2 user refers 2 more = 30 Level 3 users. You earn 5%. 30 × $48 × 5% = +$72/month more. Total passive income from 3-level network: $252/month — more than most subscriptions cost.
Our Platform
We keep 60% of all rebates even after paying all referral levels. The referral program pays for itself because it brings new users at $0 CAC — vs $17–$85 from telecalling + ads.

🧠 What Is an IB (Introducing Broker)?
An Introducing Broker (IB) is a person or company that has their own network of traders — but does not have a trading platform. They introduce their clients to our platform and earn a commission for every client who deposits. Think of them like franchise partners: they bring their existing client base, we provide the technology, AI signals, and education. Both parties earn from every deposit and every trade.
⚡ Why This Is Our Highest-Margin Channel
An IB partner might have 200 existing trader contacts. When they promote TradeGPT to those contacts, 15–30 might join with deposits of $500–$5,000 each. We earn $200 per depositing client from the broker. Zero advertising spend. Zero telecalling. Zero content creation. The IB does the work. We split the reward.
🔧 How We Recruit & Activate IB Partners
Month 1–2
Identify IBs in our network — Ex-stock brokers, Telegram trading group admins, YouTube finance creators, WhatsApp group owners with 500+ traders. Target: people who already have trust with a trading audience but no platform to monetize it.

Month 3+
Activate Their Audience — IB runs their own mini-campaign to their client base. We support with co-branded webinars, shareable content, and product demos. Their audience is already warm — conversion rates are 25–40%.
Ongoing
